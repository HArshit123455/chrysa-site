Chrysa

Make room for life.

A screen time app for Android that never blocks anything. When you open an app on autopilot, it offers something better to do than scrolling — one small thing, sized to the time you actually have.

Free on Google Play

No ads, no account, nothing to unlock.

What it does

A pause, not a wall. Choose the apps you open without deciding to. The moment one opens, Chrysa offers a two-minute alternative. You can open the app anyway — nothing is ever blocked, and “Not today” is a complete answer.

Your day, read from your phone. Sleep, steps and screen time, without typing them in. Plus the open blocks left in your afternoon or evening, and an idea for what would make one feel worthwhile.

A window, not a dashboard. Your home screen is a window. The sky is whatever hour you are really in. Outside it, the land fills with what you actually did: a tree for a walk, a lit window for an evening with someone, a bird for twenty pages.

Logging in plain words. “Walked 20 minutes with a friend at 7” is enough.

What it refuses to do

  • No score. Five areas of life shown as dots, never summed into one number.
  • No streaks. Nothing to keep up, and nothing out there decays.
  • No blocking. It makes a moment between impulse and action, then gets out of the way.
  • No subscription, no ads, nothing behind a paywall.
  • No account, and no server of ours for your data to sit on.

The best outcome we can imagine is that you open Chrysa less, because your life got better.

Privacy, plainly

Everything lives in a database on your phone. There is no account and no backend. Screen time, sleep and steps are read on the device and never leave it.

There is exactly one exception, and it is a switch you control, off by default: nearby places and weather from the internet. With it on, Chrysa sends a location rounded to about a kilometre to Open-Meteo, and to about 110 metres to OpenStreetMap — a block, because a kilometre of error is the difference between your neighbourhood and your city. Nothing else ever leaves the phone.

You can export everything as a file, or delete all of it, any time. Full privacy policy · How to delete your data

Questions

Does Chrysa block apps?

No. Chrysa never blocks anything. When you open an app you tend to open on autopilot, it offers one small alternative sized to the time you have, and you can open the app anyway.

Is Chrysa free?

Yes. Chrysa is free on Google Play, with no ads, no subscription and nothing to unlock.

Does Chrysa need an account?

No. There is no account and no server. Everything lives in a database on your phone.

Does Chrysa work offline?

Yes, entirely. The one exception is an off-by-default switch for nearby places and weather, which sends only a rounded location.

What does Chrysa track?

Sleep, steps and screen time read from the phone, plus anything you log in plain words. It shows five areas of life as dots — never one overall score, and no streaks.

Is Chrysa on iPhone?

Android only for now.
