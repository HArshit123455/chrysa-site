Delete your Chrysa data

Chrysa, by Harshit Sindhu

Chrysa keeps everything on your phone. There is no account and no server, so deleting your data is instant and complete, and you never have to ask us.

Steps

  1. Open Chrysa and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll to Privacy, plainly.
  3. Tap Delete all my data and confirm.

Or simply uninstall the app. Its database is removed with it.

What is deleted

Everything: who you said you want to become, goals, activity, meal, sleep and water logs, routines, reflections, screen-time and step records, the optional weight numbers, settings, and permission records. The app returns to its first screen.

What is kept

Nothing. There is no copy anywhere else and no retention period. If you had switched on places and weather, the only thing that ever left the phone was a location rounded to about a kilometre with no identifier attached; the public services it went to hold nothing that can be tied to you.
